An entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261121** — "Sum up
21 November's world news in one image."

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) draws a complete newspaper
front page — masthead, dateline, lead illustration and columns — pixel by
pixel in code and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (1024 × 768, PNG).
No photographs, no stock imagery, no network at run time; the output is
byte-for-byte deterministic across runs.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## The story chosen

The lead is the night's escalation of the war in Europe: **Russia fired
more than 400 drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight, and two
missiles and a suspected drone came down inside Poland** — Prime Minister
Tusk called it a "deliberate provocation" and NATO scrambled jets. The
illustration shows the missile trails arcing over Kyiv's rooftops, the
dashed border, and a fallen missile on the Polish side.

Around it, the day's other front-page stories:

- **Markets** — the AI sell-off pushed the S&P 500 into correction
  (−3.7%), erased the Nasdaq's 2026 gains, and knocked 5% off the Nikkei.
- **Justice** — fallout from the ICJ's first-ever verdict holding a state
  responsible for genocide (Myanmar, over the Rohingya); Myanmar called
  the ruling "unfair" and Britain expelled its envoy.
- **Diplomacy** — Europe's rejection of a "new Yalta" after the Shenzhen
  Trump–Xi–Putin summit: Chancellor Merz refused any carve-up of Europe,
  and Zelensky said of Putin, "He's a killer."
- In the ticker: COP31 talks deadlocked in Antalya with a draft silent
  on fossil fuels; a trial pill (daraxonrasib) clearing pancreatic
  tumours in 1 in 12 patients; BepiColombo reaching Mercury orbit after
  eight years; a magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Peru.

Headline sources relied on: the OneTurn News day digest for
21 November 2026 (aggregating France 24, CBS News, BBC News, The
Guardian, PBS NewsHour, UPI and Al Jazeera), Reuters and CNN/Human
Rights Watch on the ICJ ruling and Myanmar's response, and Europe Says /
EU Today on Chancellor Merz's "new Yalta" rejection.
